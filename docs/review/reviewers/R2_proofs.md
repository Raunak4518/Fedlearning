# Reviewer 2: proof audit (optimisation, stochastic algorithms, theoretical ML)

**Specialty.** Convex and stochastic optimisation, adaptive methods (Adam), optimal transport and moment problems, kernel methods (MMD), and differential-privacy accounting.

**Recommendation.** *Major revision.* The new triangle-inequality theorem holds. The Adam scale-invariance lemma holds on its stated domain, and I quantify where that domain ends. The two moment-correction optimality results (Prop 4, Prop 6) and the zCDP accounting are correct. Five problems remain:

- Theorem 1 is a per-round identity built from measured quantities. Its "threshold" θ_c is endogenous, so it predicts nothing.
- The claim "a class held by every client cannot be contracted by the aggregation" is false. The authors' own T01 log shows contractions at m_c = K.
- The earlier K/2 bound (Prop 1, earlier form) is false, with counterexamples of size d=2, K=1, S=1.
- The deployed MC and MC-S maps (ReLU, clamping, shrinkage, plug-in moments, KH re-selection) do not satisfy the constraints under which Props 4 and 6 are optimal. The text nevertheless says MC "fixes exactly" the moments.
- Lemma 1 (KH risk bound) is vacuous for the heads used. Losses of ReLU networks, and even constants, are not in a Gaussian RKHS. The RFF RKHS is a span of D cosines.
- Prop 3's variance term is the subsampling model, not the stated weighting model. The closed form w* changes, but the qualitative conclusions survive.

**Confidence.** High (4/5) on every item marked "demonstrated". Medium on items marked "suspected".

## 5. Questions for the authors

1. Theorem 1 is an identity in measured norms. Can you give a model of Ḡ(r) and D̄(r), for example holder Adam steps as a function of the class-in-batch probability and of ‖r‖, that predicts the equilibrium tail-row norm *before* training? Does it match the 4–10% observed?
2. Please withdraw or correct the statement that a class held by all clients cannot be contracted. How do you explain classes 3–4 (m_c = 10) contracting in T01?
3. At what λ does the observed D̄ start to depend on λ in T01 (try 1e-6 and 1e-7)? S1 predicts a drop of about 6% and 42%.
4. Report the MC-corrected samples' **post-ReLU, post-KH** class means and spreads against the targets (m_c, V_c). How often do the clamp [0.1, 10] and the eigenvalue clip activate?
5. For Lemma 1, which RKHS norm bound B do you claim for a CE loss of a ReLU head? Would you replace it with an approximation-error version, or with a W₁ or Lipschitz bound?
6. Prop 3: is λ a sample weight or a subsampling fraction? If a weight, will you adopt w* = (σ²/n_r)/(σ²/n_r + σ²/n_s + b²)?
7. DP: which neighbouring relation (add/remove or replace) is used, who adds the noise under secure aggregation, and is the held-out split used for clipping disjoint from all client data and genuinely public?
8. The SVHN separation argument uses pixel-space F. What are √(2F) and the "18×" factor in the 3×16×16 feature space the generator actually models?
9. Under HWA, how many tail-row contractions per run come from holder cancellation versus negative holder Δ_k?
